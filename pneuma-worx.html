About Pneuma Worx

Pneuma Worx is a professional consulting practice based in Wellington, New Zealand. We specialize in helping organizations achieve their strategic objectives through thoughtful consulting, expert project management, and organizational development.

Our approach emphasizes clarity, collaboration, and delivery. We work closely with our clients to understand their unique challenges and opportunities, then apply our expertise to create practical, sustainable solutions.

Whether you're planning a strategic initiative, managing a complex project, or building organizational capability, we're here to help your organization breathe and thrive.
